FluidVoice Dictation
Talk into any text box on the web

How to use

  1. Click in a text box on any website.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+D, or click the small microphone in the box, and talk.
  3. Press the shortcut again, or Done. Your words are typed where the cursor is.
Say “comma”, “period” or “new line” for punctuation.
